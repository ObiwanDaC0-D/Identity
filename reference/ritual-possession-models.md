# Ritual, Invocation and Possession Archive

26 comparative models spanning ceremonial practices, possession, spirit communication, deity identification, psychological interpretation and sociological theory. This is a curated learning reference, not an exhaustive systematic review. Source links are bibliographic searches and must be checked before formal citation. Describing a religious interpretation does not verify or disprove its metaphysical claims. Possession does not inherently imply pathology.

**Domain:** Ceremonial

**Scholar or literature:** T. M. Luhrmann

**Research question:** Inspiration, identification, or takeover?
