## Analytical questions

For each framework ask: What is the unit of analysis? How is identity formed and changed? What mechanism is proposed? What evidence supports it? How are power, context and culture handled?

**Distinctions:** Social identity theory is not the same as sociological identity theory. Butler's performativity is not synonymous with consciously acting a role. Embodied cognition is a family of theories, not one uniform claim.
