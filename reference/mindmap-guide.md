# Interactive Theory Mind Map

The standalone `mindmap.html` displays **28 existing frameworks** and **8 comparative ritual concepts**, including invocation, evocation, spirit possession, mediumship, Drawing Down, deity identification, trance/absorption and ritual recognition.

## Interaction
- Select a node to inspect its description, research question and conceptual relationships.
- Drag the background to pan; use wheel/pinch or zoom controls; reset to the original view.
- Search by theory, author or concept; filter by identity, acting or ritual.
- Keyboard users can focus nodes and activate them with Enter or Space.
- Main Atlas and Research Studio link to the map, with a mobile bottom-navigation destination.

## Interpretive safeguards
The edges represent **editorial, comparative connections**, not causal findings, empirically proven relationships, or equivalence between traditions. Ritual concepts are distinguished from the 28 indexed scholarly frameworks. Religious explanations are described as interpretations rather than verified supernatural claims.
