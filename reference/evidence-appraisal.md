# Evidence Appraisal — V9

The research library filters source types within each theory. Categories are **Experimental**, **Observational / empirical**, **Review / synthesis**, **Theory / conceptual**, and **Foundational / interpretive**. They are design descriptors, not quality rankings.

For each source, evaluate sampling, construct validity, research design, effect size and uncertainty (where relevant), competing explanations, replication and generalizability. An experimental study is not automatically strong evidence; an important philosophical or pedagogical work is not automatically empirical validation.

**Citation export:** Copy citation provides the displayed citation and link. Copy BibTeX produces a minimal `@misc` record from the available metadata. Verify titles, author formatting, venues and years before formal scholarly use. The library is curated, not a systematic literature review.
